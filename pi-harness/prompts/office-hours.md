argument-hint: "[decision or feature]"
---
Run a product/engineering interrogation on: $ARGUMENTS

Ask forcing questions ONE AT A TIME, adapting to my answers:
1. Who exactly is this for and what do they do today instead?
2. What is the narrowest version that delivers real value?
3. What does success concretely look like (observable behavior)?
4. What are we explicitly NOT doing?
5. What would make this approach obviously wrong in 3 months?

Then present at least 2 alternative approaches (one minimal, one ideal) with tradeoffs and your recommendation. HARD RULE: write zero code until I pick a direction. Challenge weak premises directly; no sycophancy.
