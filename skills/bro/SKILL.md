# Bro

Restate your previous message in plain human language:

- Zero jargon, zero acronyms left unexplained by plain words.
- Same meaning, no new claims, nothing dropped that matters.
- Shorter and simpler than the original.
- One human talking to another, out loud.

If a technical term is unavoidable, name the thing itself instead of the term. No analogies unless needed to carry meaning.
